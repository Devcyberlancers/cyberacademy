FAQs

Common queries regarding training slots, fees, and tests.

Is the entrance exam mandatory?

Yes, all candidates must take our Phase-1 assessment. Training batches are assigned based on exam score and qualification parameters.

What is the refund policy?

The 500 RS entrance test fee is non-refundable. For course fees, full refunds (excluding GST) are available if requested within 7 days of the batch start date.

Are the lab sessions physical or online?

Both. The Phygital Lab facilities offer hands-on local range access in Hyderabad as well as simulated digital terminal endpoints remotely.

Can I shift my training slot?

Subject to slot availability, students can request to shift between Morning and Afternoon batches during the first 2 weeks.
