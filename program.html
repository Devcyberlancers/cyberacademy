FAQ

Common questions.

Is CSIC a red-team hacking course?

No. CSIC is positioned around defensive cybersecurity: SOC, SIEM, threat hunting, forensics, and incident response.

Is the program online or offline?

The current program is listed as offline in Mysuru.

Are the fees final?

The published tiers are Rs. 59,000, Rs. 1,19,000, and Rs. 2,00,000. GST treatment and tier inclusions still need final confirmation.
